# Gromoze CRM

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Platform administrators operate the SaaS platform. Customer organizations have
their own tenant workspaces and branding.

## Product Purpose

Real estate CRM for leads, follow-ups, site visits, and sales.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current application uses React, Vite, shared UI components, and existing
password and OTP authentication. The login redesign is confirmed to apply only
to the Gromoze platform login. Preserve tenant login layouts and branding,
authentication behavior, and the existing no-remember-me policy.

## Brand Commitments

Use the approved Gromoze logo unchanged, existing Manrope typography and theme
tokens, and the user's supplied login composition and architectural photograph.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied a login design reference and its separate background image.
The unchanged background is stored at
`src/assets/platform-login-background.png`; its source is the user's
`Golden Hour Apartment Boulevard.png` file.
The right-side curve is the user's supplied `Deep Blue Curved Wave Panel.png`,
stored at `src/assets/platform-login-wave.png`; retain its original contour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile layouts,
and existing accessible field and action names.
